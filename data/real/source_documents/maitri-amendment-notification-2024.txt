1. Short title and commencement.— (1) This Act may be called the Maharashtra Industry, Trade and Investment Facilitation (Amendment) Act, 2024. (2) It shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette.

2. Definitions.— In the principal Act, in section 2,— (a) for clause (g), the following clause shall be substituted, namely:— "(g) \"Nodal Agency\" means the Maharashtra Industry, Trade and Investment Promotion Council (MAITRI Council) established under section 14;"; (b) in clause (m), for the words "specified time limit", the words "specified statutory time limit not exceeding twenty-one days" shall be substituted.

3. Filing of application.— In section 3 of the principal Act, for sub-section (2), the following sub-section shall be substituted, namely:— "(2) Any such application under sub-section (1) shall be submitted exclusively in digital form through the Single Window System without requiring physical submission of paper documents: Provided that micro, small and medium enterprises shall be exempt from application processing fees for statutory clearances."

4. Disposal of application.— In section 4 of the principal Act,— (a) in sub-section (2), for the words "within specified time limit", the words "only once within seven working days of application submission" shall be substituted; (b) for sub-section (3), the following sub-section shall be substituted, namely:— "(3) The Competent Authority shall take decision on such application within the specified statutory time limit of twenty-one days, failing which deemed approval shall be generated automatically by the Single Window System."

14. Nodal Agency.— In section 14 of the principal Act, for sub-section (1), the following sub-section shall be substituted, namely:— "(1) The Maharashtra Industry, Trade and Investment Promotion Council (MAITRI Council) shall be the autonomous Nodal Agency for the Single Window System in the State of Maharashtra, vested with statutory powers of coordination and inter-departmental oversight."

16. Rationalization of inspections.— In section 16 of the principal Act, the following sub-sections shall be added, namely:— "(2) Joint site inspections for industrial undertakings falling in Red and Orange categories shall be conducted synchronously by all concerned authorities within fifteen days from the date of application. (3) The joint inspection report shall be uploaded on the MAITRI portal within forty-eight hours of inspection completion, failing which the inspection shall be deemed approved with zero non-compliances."

19. Power to levy fees.— In section 19 of the principal Act, the following proviso shall be inserted, namely:— "Provided that women-owned manufacturing units and micro enterprises registered under the MSMED Act shall be exempt from the payment of facilitation fees under this section."

20. Online Wizard Module and Single Window Integration.— In section 20 of the principal Act, for sub-section (4), the following sub-section shall be substituted, namely:— "(4) Every Competent Authority shall integrate all statutory permission workflows with the Online Wizard Module within thirty days of notification, failing which disciplinary proceedings under section 12 shall be initiated."
