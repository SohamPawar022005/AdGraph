1. Short title, extent and commencement.— (1) This Act may be called the Maharashtra Industrial Development (Amendment) Act, 2012. (2) It shall come into force at once on publication in the Official Gazette.

14. Functions of the Corporation.— In section 14 of the principal Act, in clause (ii), the following sub-clauses shall be added, namely:— "(c) establish, finance, and operate common effluent treatment plants (CETP) and green buffer zones across all special industrial estates; (d) plan and develop smart integrated industrial townships equipped with single-window utility connections."

42A. Acquisition of unutilised surplus lands in industrial areas.— In section 42A of the principal Act, for sub-section (1), the following sub-section shall be substituted, namely:— "(1) The Corporation shall conduct a quarterly audit of all land parcels allotted in industrial areas and submit a report within ninety days on unutilised lands. (2) Any industrial plot remaining unutilised for a period exceeding three years from the date of possession shall be liable to resumption by the Corporation after giving sixty days statutory notice."

46. Penalty for construction or use of land and buildings contrary to terms of holding.— In section 46 of the principal Act, in sub-section (2), for the words "fine which may extend to five thousand rupees", the words "fine which shall not be less than twenty-five thousand rupees and may extend to one lakh rupees, and in the case of continuing contravention, with a daily fine of one thousand rupees" shall be substituted.

48. Powers of entry and inspection.— In section 48 of the principal Act, for the words "Any officer of the State Government", the words "Any authorized officer of the Corporation acting jointly with an inspector of the Maharashtra Pollution Control Board" shall be substituted.
